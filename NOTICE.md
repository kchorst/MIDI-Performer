## Third-party components

MIDI Performer can locate, download, or invoke third-party components. Those components are not relicensed by MIDI Performer.

### FluidSynth

Cross-platform software synthesizer based on the SoundFont specification.

Project: <https://github.com/FluidSynth/fluidsynth>

### GeneralUser GS

General MIDI and GS-compatible SoundFont. When downloaded through MIDI Performer, its license is stored beside the SoundFont.

Project: <https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS>

### Audiveris

Optical music recognition software used optionally for score analysis.

Project: <https://github.com/Audiveris/audiveris>

### FFmpeg

Multimedia framework used optionally for compressed audio input and MP3 export.

Project: <https://ffmpeg.org/>

### Mutopia Project sample

The Maple Leaf Rag helper downloads a Mutopia edition identified by Mutopia as public domain. Source details and a checksum are written beside the downloaded MIDI.

Source page: <https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=23>
